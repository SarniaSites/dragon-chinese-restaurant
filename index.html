    Good to know

    Before you book or order.

    Opening times
    Every evening from 5pm, except Tuesdays.
    Paying
    Card payments from £13. We don't take cheques or American Express.
    Booking
    For tonight, ring 01481 244 688. For another day, call or use the form below.
    Allergens
    Some dishes contain celery, gluten, crustaceans, egg, fish, lupin, milk, molluscs, mustard, nuts, peanuts, sesame, soya or sulphites. Tell us about any allergy before you order and we'll help you choose.
